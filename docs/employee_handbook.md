# Larkspur Labs Employee Handbook - Overview

Welcome to Larkspur Labs. This handbook summarises the policies that apply to all
full-time employees. It is a fictional document created for a demo project. Where this
handbook and your employment contract disagree, the contract wins. Questions go to
people-ops@larkspurlabs.example.

# Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accruing
at 1.5 days per month. PTO requests of three or more consecutive days must be submitted
in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Up to 5 unused PTO days roll over into
the next calendar year; any balance above that is forfeited on 31 December. In addition
to PTO, Larkspur Labs observes 11 public holidays per year, published each January.

# Remote Work and Office Hours

Larkspur Labs is remote-first. Employees may work from any location in a country where
the company has a legal entity (United States, United Kingdom, Germany and India). Core
collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone, during which you
are expected to be reachable on Slack. Working from a different country for more than
30 days in a rolling 12-month period requires written approval from people-ops.

# Expenses and Equipment

Every new hire receives a laptop and a one-time home office stipend of USD 600, claimable
in the first 90 days of employment. Recurring expenses such as co-working desks are
reimbursed up to USD 250 per month with a receipt. Expense claims must be filed in Expensify
within 30 days of the purchase date; claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed. Travel
must be booked through the TravelPerk portal, and economy class is the default for flights
under eight hours.

# Parental Leave

Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, and secondary
caregivers to 6 weeks, both usable within the first 12 months after birth or adoption.
Leave can be taken in up to two separate blocks. Employees must have completed their
probation period (3 months) to be eligible. Statutory leave in your country applies where
it is more generous.

# Performance Reviews and Promotions

Performance reviews run twice a year, in March and September. Each review combines a
self-assessment, manager feedback and feedback from two peers. Promotion decisions are
made in the September cycle only, and salary adjustments from promotions take effect on
1 October. Engineering levels run from L1 (Associate Engineer) to L6 (Principal Engineer).
